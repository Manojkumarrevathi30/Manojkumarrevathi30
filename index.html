I am a Data Engineer at Systech Solutions Inc. with 2 years of experience working on enterprise data engineering solutions, primarily in the insurance domain.

        
I work on the Pacific Life insurance project, contributing to data integration, transformation and orchestration workflows. My hands-on experience includes Python, SQL, Snowflake, dbt, Matillion, Control-M and Salesforce data integration.

        
I enjoy designing and developing reliable data pipelines, transforming complex datasets into business-ready information, implementing data quality processes and automating data workflows.

        
Beyond my project responsibilities, I have worked on internal analytics initiatives involving synthetic data generation and dashboard development, as well as end-to-end data engineering simulation projects.

        
I am also deeply interested in exploring AI-assisted engineering, intelligent workflow automation and emerging data technologies to improve development efficiency and operational reliability.

        
My professional focus is building scalable, maintainable and business-driven data solutions while continuously evolving as a Data Engineer.
